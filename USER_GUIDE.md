## 📝 Creating Content

### Creating Posts
1. Go to **Home** page
2. Click "What's on your mind?"
3. Type your post
4. Optionally use **AI Suggestions**:
   - Click "✨ AI Ideas"
   - Get content suggestions
   - Click to insert
5. Add images (optional)
6. Click "Post"

### Uploading Videos (TikTok-Style)
1. Click **Videos** in navigation
2. Click **"Upload Video"** or drag-and-drop
3. Select video file (MP4, WebM, etc.)
4. Fill in details:
   - **Title**: "My awesome tech review"
   - **Description**: "Short explanation"
   - **Category**: Tutorial, Demo, Review, or News
   - **Tags**: tech, review, etc.
5. Click **"Publish"**
6. Share your video!

**Tips for Video Success**:
- Keep under 5 minutes
- Use good lighting
- Clear audio
- Hook viewers in first 3 seconds
- Add captions for accessibility

### Creating Marketplace Listings
1. Go to **Marketplace**
2. Click **"Sell Item"**
3. Fill in details:
   - **Title**: Be specific (e.g., "iPhone 14 Pro Max, 256GB, Space Black")
   - **Category**: Electronics, Software, Services, etc.
   - **Price**: Set your price
   - **Condition**: New, Like New, Used, Refurbished
   - **Description**: Full details about item
   - **Location**: Where you are/shipping from
   - **Photos**: Upload 3-5 photos (different angles)
4. Click **"Create Listing"**
5. Buyers will inquire about your item

**Marketplace Tips**:
- Multiple photos increase sales
- Clear descriptions = more inquiries
- Respond quickly to buyers
- Competitive pricing gets more attention
- High ratings = more future sales

## 🤖 Using AI Features

### AI Settings & Configuration
1. Go to `/ai-settings.html`
2. Enter your Groq API key (get free at https://console.groq.com/)
3. Choose your preferred AI model:
   - **Mixtral 8x7B**: Fastest, best for most tasks
   - **LLaMA 2 70B**: Most powerful, slower
   - **Gemma 7B**: Lightweight, quick

### AI Writing Assistant
1. **Generate Content Ideas**: Click "✨ Ideas" on post editor
2. **Improve Writing**: Select text → "Improve" → AI enhances it
3. **SEO Optimization**: Help optimize titles/descriptions for search
4. **Code Help**: Paste code → AI helps debug and improve
5. **Moderation Check**: Ensure content is appropriate before posting

### How It Works
- AI runs **locally** in your browser
- Your API key **never** goes to Timzee servers
- Your data stays **private**
- Tokens are counted from your Groq account

### Example Prompts
- "Generate 5 tech blog post ideas"
- "Improve this paragraph for clarity"
- "Help me debug this JavaScript"
- "Create SEO title for my article"

3. **Search** for specific items
4. Click any listing to see:

- Use Timzee's secure payment (coming soon)

## ✅ Getting Verified

### Why Get Verified?
- **Silver Badge**: Custom profile, increased visibility
- **Gold Badge**: Premium features, featured in creator directory
- **Platinum Badge**: Exclusive features, 24/7 support, homepage feature
- **Business Badge**: Business profile, analytics, custom branding

### How to Apply
1. Go to `/verify.html`
2. Select verification level
3. Explain why you deserve it:
   - Your contributions
   - Your expertise
   - Your community impact
4. Upload supporting documents:
   - ID or business license
   - Portfolio or samples
   - Press mentions (screenshots)
   - Social media followers proof
5. Submit application
6. Wait for review (3-5 business days)
7. Get notified of decision

### Requirements by Level
- **Silver**: 50+ posts, active community member
- **Gold**: 500+ followers, verified expertise
- **Platinum**: 5000+ followers, industry leadership
- **Business**: Business registration, official website

## 💬 Messaging & Notifications

### Real-Time Messaging
1. Go to **Chat** (Messages)
2. Select a conversation
3. Type your message
4. **No page refresh needed!** Messages appear instantly
5. See when others are typing
6. Get notifications for new messages

### Notification Types
You'll be notified when:
- ✉️ **New Message**: Someone sends you a DM
- 👤 **Friend Request**: Someone wants to connect
- 💬 **Comment Reply**: Someone replies to your comment
- @️ **Mention**: Someone mentions you in a post
- ✓ **Verification Status**: Your verification decision
- 🎖️ **Admin Promotion**: You're promoted to moderator

### Managing Notifications
- Click **notification bell** in header
- See all recent notifications
- Click to go to that item
- Mark as read
- Clear old notifications

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Search Isn't Working
- **Solution 1**: Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
- **Solution 2**: Try different search term (min 2 characters)
- **Solution 3**: Refresh page and try again
- **Contact**: Go to `/contact.html` if problem persists

### Messages Need Refresh
- **Solution**: We've fixed this! Update browser cache
- **If still happening**: Clear localStorage, logout and login again

### AI Features Not Working
- **Check**: Did you enter your Groq API key?
- **Verify**: Is API key valid? Test in ai-settings.html
- **Ensure**: You have Groq account with credits
- **Update**: Refresh browser tab

### Videos Won't Upload
- **Check**: File size (max 500MB recommended)
- **Verify**: Format is MP4 or WebM
- **Free**: Disk space on computer
- **Try**: Chrome instead of Safari

### Marketplace Listings Not Showing
- **Wait**: Database syncs might take 5-10 seconds
- **Refresh**: Browser (F5)
- **Check**: Are you using right category filter?
- **Logout**: And back in to reset state

### Can't Get Verified
- **Check**: Do you meet minimum requirements?
- **Upload**: Clear supporting documents
- **Write**: Compelling reason (at least 20 characters)
- **Patience**: Review takes 3-5 business days

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

### Protect Your Account
- Use **strong password** (12+ chars, mix of types)
- **Don't share** password with anyone
- **Enable** notifications for login attempts
- **Review** connected apps regularly
- **Logout** on shared devices

### Privacy Settings
- Go to **Profile → Settings**
- Choose **Public** or **Private** profile
- Public = anyone can view
- Private = friends only
- Control who can DM you
- Manage data sharing

### Reporting Issues
- **Report User**: Click ⋯ menu, select "Report"
- **Report Content**: "Report post" from menu
- **Inappropriate Content**: Use quick flag button
- **Harassment**: Report to admin team
- **Scams**: Alert community + Timzee team
