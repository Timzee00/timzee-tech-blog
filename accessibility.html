Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Timzee Tech Hub is being developed with keyboard access, readable controls, descriptive labels, responsive layouts, and alternative text for meaningful images in mind. Accessibility is treated as an ongoing engineering requirement rather than a one-time checkbox.

Current accessibility practices

The interface uses semantic headings and landmarks, visible focus states, accessible names for icon controls, skip navigation, responsive media sizing, and form labels or placeholders appropriate to the feature.

Known limitations

Some older pages and third-party content may still need refinement. A visual or browser-specific issue can also appear before a fix reaches every deployment.

Report an issue

Tell us which page, control, browser/device, and action caused difficulty through Contact Us. Screenshots or a short description can help us reproduce the issue.
