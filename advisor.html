Blipmood staff review these applications. False qualifications, conflicts of interest or misuse may result in rejection or revocation.

How it works

  1. Build a real history of constructive contributions.
  2. Apply with an honest explanation and relevant evidence.
  3. Blipmood staff evaluate your expertise and fit.
  4. If approved, your title and specialization appear in Account Transparency.
  5. Continued participation and conduct are reviewed; appointments can be withdrawn.
