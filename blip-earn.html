How referrals work

A referral must be a real new, email-verified adult account that finishes onboarding and genuinely interacts on at least three distinct days. Staff reviews each referral before it counts toward a milestone. Self-referrals, purchased accounts and automated signups do not qualify.

When the founder pauses the program or the verified-member target is reached, new referrals stop. Existing records and previously approved rewards remain. Rewards are requested here but fulfilled manually by the founder via the official verified chat.
