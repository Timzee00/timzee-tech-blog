Cookie Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

What we use

The site uses a small set of first-party cookies and browser storage for essential session behavior, security, interface preferences and your cookie choices. Optional measurement cookies are not enabled until you accept optional cookies.

Cookie categories

Essential: required for core account/session and security behavior.

Preference: remembers choices such as consent and selected product preferences where supported.

Measurement: reserved for optional usage measurement features. The current site only records an opt-in flag when you accept optional cookies; it does not silently load a third-party marketing pixel.

Your choice

You can accept or reject optional cookies from the privacy banner. You can reopen the control at any time below. Rejecting optional cookies does not remove essential storage required for requested features.

Third-party content
