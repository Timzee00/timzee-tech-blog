## Manual verification before public rollout
1. Search exact `TIMZEE`, `TIM ZEE`, `TIMZY`, `TIMZEE TECH`, sound-alikes, and proposed stylized marks at
   https://branddb.wipo.int/ and Nigeria's registry
   https://www.nipo.gov.ng/trademark.jsp .
   Check likely Nice classes 9 (apps/software), 35 (marketplace/advertising),
   38 (communications/social services), 41 (publishing/education),
   42 (SaaS/platform software); a local trademark professional can refine.
2. Check CAC business-name/company registration separately. It does **not**
   substitute for a trademark search.
3. Check registrar/WHOIS/RDAP and checkout for `timzee.com`, `timzee.ng`,
   `timzee.app`, `jointimzee.com` and `timzee.social`. No claim that any
   are currently available, priced reasonably or owned by us.
4. Check direct `@timzee` and `@jointimzee` handles on X, Instagram,
   TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Threads, Bluesky and GitHub.
   Social search snippets and generic handle checkers do not prove availability.
5. Check Google Play/App Store name collisions and branded search results.
6. Keep screenshots, search dates, registrar quotes, existing-use evidence and
   legal review notes. Re-run immediately before registering/buying.

## Decision gates
- **GREEN:** suitable trademark review, verified claimable domain and social
  identity, no unacceptable conflict, approved internal rollout.
- **AMBER:** mixed or unavailable exact handles; use a documented consistent
  alternate, but do not imply `@timzee` or `timzee.com` is secured.
- **RED:** material pre-existing rights or serious confusion risk; suspend
  customer-facing rename, iterate brand name before changing domains/legal notices.
