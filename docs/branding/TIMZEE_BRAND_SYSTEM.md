# Timzee — brand system v0.1 (PROPOSED, NOT LEGALLY CLEARED)
Updated 2026-10-20. This is a *staging specification* and does not change
the production brand, registered business/operator or domain.

## Architecture
- Customer brand: **Timzee**
- Product descriptor (for SEO and transition): **Timzee — community, creators and technology**
- Suggested tagline: **Explore. Create. Connect.**
- Position: a welcoming home for curious people to learn, discuss, publish, share, connect and discover.
- Brand promise: practical discovery, trustworthy communities and creator ownership.
- Communication voice: welcoming, direct, curious, international, not elitist or hyperbolic.
- Transition banner (first 60–90 days): **Timzee Tech Hub is becoming Timzee. Your account, posts and communities are staying right here.**
- Legal operator remains **Timzee Corp** *only if that is the actual legal entity*; confirm legal ownership before publishing corporate claims. A brand nickname never changes contractual counterpart by itself.

## Original visual direction (no copied brand assets)
- Wordmark: lowercase `timzee`, approachable geometric sans, strong legibility at 16px.
- Mark: an original abstract joined **t/z** exploration symbol, built with simple vector paths. Avoid shapes confusingly similar to TikTok, Discord, Meta, Threads or an existing Timzee watch-store theme.
- Primary: deep ocean `#12363F`; secondary: rich teal `#087F73`; accent: vibrant mint `#48DBC0`.
- Neutrals: cloud `#F5F8F8`, ink `#142D35`, night `#0B171D`, white `#FFFFFF`.
- Accessibility: WCAG AA text contrast; do not use mint for small text on white without checking contrast; honor reduced motion.
- Fonts: system-ui first, no downloaded proprietary font embedded in source; avoid layout shift.
- Components: verification tiers keep their established silver/gold/platinum/organization identity colors while surrounding UI uses Timzee teal.
- Graphic styles: rounded 12–16px panels, sparse gradients, clear focus rings, no heavy full-screen animations.
- Photo sourcing: original/licensed media only; attribution visible for creator content where applicable.
