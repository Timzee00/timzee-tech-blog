BLIPMOOD ON YOUR DEVICE

Take Blipmood with you.

Install Blipmood on your home screen for a focused, app-like experience. Choose your device below.

ANDROID

Install on Android

Add Blipmood from a supported browser. Your account, messages and content continue to use the same secure Blipmood website.

Open this page in Chrome, tap the browser menu (⋮), then choose Install app or Add to Home screen when available.

This installs the web app. A separate Android APK is not published yet.

IPHONE

Add to iPhone

Use Safari to add Blipmood to your Home Screen. iPhone installation does not use an APK.

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. Scroll down if needed.
  4. When shown, enable Open as Web App, then tap Add.

The Blipmood icon will appear on your Home Screen. Availability of individual options can vary by iOS version and region.
