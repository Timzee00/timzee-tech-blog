Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Timzee Tech Hub is a community platform operated by Timzee Corp. This policy explains the main categories of information the service may handle and the choices available to users.

Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, this may include account details, profile information, posts, comments, messages, marketplace listings, support requests, and technical information needed to operate the service.

Why we use it

We use information to create accounts, provide requested features, keep conversations and community content working, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, improve reliability, and meet applicable obligations.

Messages and community content

Content you publish or send is used to provide the feature you requested. Private messages are intended for the participants of the relevant chat, subject to account permissions, moderation, security operations, and applicable law.

Cookies and local storage

Essential session and preference storage may be required for the site to work. Optional measurement or similar technologies should only be enabled where the site has provided the applicable choice. See the Cookie Policy.

Sharing and service providers

We may use infrastructure and service providers that process data on our behalf, such as hosting, database, authentication, messaging, storage, email, or AI service providers when those features are enabled. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

Retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary for the feature, security, dispute handling, legal obligations, and other legitimate operational needs. Different data types may have different retention periods.

Your choices and rights

You can contact us about access, correction, deletion, account questions, privacy concerns, or consent choices. Some rights may depend on applicable law and the specific circumstances of the request.

Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so keep your credentials private and report suspicious activity promptly.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact Us page and describe the request clearly.
