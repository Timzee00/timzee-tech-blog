Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Timzee Tech Hub may provide marketplace listings, paid services, or other transactions depending on the features enabled at the time. The refund path depends on who charged you and what was purchased.

Member-to-member marketplace transactions

Where a listing only connects a buyer with an independent seller, the seller is responsible for the transaction terms, fulfilment, returns, and any refund they offer. Contact the seller first and keep relevant transaction records.

Direct Timzee purchases

Where Timzee Corp directly charges you for a product or service, the transaction page or invoice may specify cancellation and refund terms. We will handle eligible requests according to the disclosed terms and applicable consumer rights.

How to request help

Use Contact Us with the account email, relevant listing or order reference, date, and a clear explanation of the issue. Do not send payment-card numbers or passwords in a message.

Statutory rights

Nothing in this page is intended to remove consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. Specific refund and cancellation rights can depend on the transaction, product, service, and applicable law.
