What do the statuses mean?
Waiting to send
The message is queued but Brevo hasn't accepted it yet.
Being processed
The scheduled worker is attempting delivery.
Accepted by Brevo
Brevo received the request; actual delivery is not guaranteed.
Delivered
Brevo reports the recipient's email system accepted it.
Needs review
A potentially uncertain send was held, preventing accidental duplicate emails.
Skipped safely
Sending was prevented by safety, consent or eligibility rules.
