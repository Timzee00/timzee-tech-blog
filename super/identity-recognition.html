Checking your super-administrator permissions…

These controls make audited decisions. Only authenticated super administrators can write records. Company affiliation requests remain private until their recipient accepts, and recognition does not grant moderation powers.

1 · Register organization

Adding a company never verifies it. First create a pending record, then assess evidence in the review panel.

2 · Verify or revoke organization

Document the evidence checked. Verified status is granted only through a reason-required, audited server transaction.

3 · Invite company affiliate

Send a pending affiliation to an existing member. It appears publicly only after the member accepts.

4 · Appoint trusted contributor

Product Lead and Product Advisor are formal appointments. Community Advisors require demonstrated expertise. Appointments do not carry admin permissions.

5 · Award recognition

Staff Pick is an editorial award, Featured is discovery placement, and Platinum is a distinguished achievement — none grants verification automatically.

6 · Inspect and revoke

Look up a member's active appointments and recognitions. Revocation updates public information and writes an audit entry.

7 · Review Advisor applications

Members can apply through Advisory Network. Review the applicant's expertise and supporting evidence before appointing them. Approval is transactional and audited.
