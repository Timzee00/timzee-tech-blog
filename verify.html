Earn Blue Verification

Verification application and review are free. Active participation helps establish your account history, but likes, follower counts and reputation points alone cannot prove authenticity or guarantee approval.

Sign in to see your account age and community reputation.
  1. Build a real history of thoughtful posts and helpful community activity.
  2. Keep your account in good standing, secure it, and use an authentic profile.
  3. Apply for the Blue Individual check and provide enough evidence to verify your account. Private documents are never public.
  4. Staff reviewers check the evidence and issue a decision. For questions or disputes, contact support.

Staff Pick awards, company roles and paid creator features do not automatically award a blue badge.

Choose an application category

Grey verification is restricted to independently reviewed government bodies and genuine public officeholders. It cannot be self-selected or earned by expressing political opinions.

Existing creator entitlements are preserved. Silver verification is now Blue, while Gold and Platinum retain their own distinct badges. Official organizations use Gold, and approved public institutions and officials use Grey.
